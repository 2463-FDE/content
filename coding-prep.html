Why this exists: clients increasingly gate interviews behind coding challenges — some live, some as take-home screens before any interview happens, even for proven engineers. 🎯 The bar

Revature's FDE benchmark is a partially solved Intermediate with at least 70% of test cases passing. You are not being asked to be a competitive programmer — clean, talked-through Entry-Level solves plus honest progress on Intermediates clears the bar.

🗣️ The method

Solve out loud with UMPIRE — it's how you solve correctly: Understand & Match find the right pattern instead of a guess, and Review & Evaluate catch the failing test cases before you submit. That's what gets you to ≥70% and beyond. Bonus: it's the exact muscle live interviews and the AI Interview grade.
