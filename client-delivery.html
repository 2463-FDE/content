How the client-work arc runs

The healthcare and finance packets above are the proven pattern. For the current client, the same pattern becomes a two-project arc with one shared storyline: learners modernize a legacy system solo, then a team inherits that system as a platform engagement. Domain choices remain trainer-owned and evidence-gated.

Project 1 · Weeks 1–5 · solo

Legacy modernization

Each learner works a brownfield system alone for a fictional US property & casualty insurance carrier — discovery, findings, and focused increments, in the same ask-per-week rhythm as the packets above.

Project 2 · Weeks 6–10 · team of 2–3

Platform engagement

A fictional US regional banking / payments firm inherits the Project 1 system. Teams of 2–3 developers carry it to delivery: platform work, handover, and a final showcase. A US utility is the cross-industry alternative for this overlay, pending trainer confirmation.

  • One shared truth: both projects tell one client storyline — personas, constraints, and artifacts stay consistent across the handover, with negotiable asks separated from hard constraints.
  • Cloud choice: AWS, GCP, or both, chosen once at the top of the curriculum; the material expects most learners to focus on a single cloud.
  • Communication: persona chat and the delayed stakeholder email are design-stage concepts — no backend, scheduler, or email machinery exists; communication coaching is formative only, never graded.
