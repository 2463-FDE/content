## Reviewed claims

- Terraform dependency graphs and plans can order declared resource relationships, but provider configurations and resource semantics remain provider-specific. Multiple provider configurations use aliases and explicit module passing. — [Provider configuration](https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/language/providers/configuration), [Providers within modules](https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/language/modules/develop/providers)
- `terraform destroy` is an apply-mode operation that removes managed objects; this curriculum does not execute it. A teardown/rebuild drill here is a paper/local rehearsal with preconditions, exclusions, evidence and stop conditions. — [Destroy command](https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/cli/commands/destroy)
- Ansible dynamic inventory uses inventory plugins and provider collections. The source specifically assigns `google.cloud` dynamic inventory as a comparison; it is not presented as a cloud-neutral inventory abstraction. — [Dynamic inventory](https://docs.ansible.com/ansible/latest/inventory_guide/intro_dynamic_inventory.html), [google.cloud collection](https://docs.ansible.com/ansible/latest/collections/google/cloud/index.html)
- Terraform plan/apply evidence, Ansible change summaries, tests, approvals and rollback records are distinct artifacts. A green job alone does not prove the client outcome or safe rollback.
