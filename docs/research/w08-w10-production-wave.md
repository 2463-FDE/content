## Production contract

Every day keeps a required reading, decision diagram with text alternative, objective-aligned interaction, and comprehension check inside 30 minutes. Required prose stays at or below 1,800 words and the displayed reading estimate is `round(words / 150)`. Optional depth is only `details.deep[data-core="optional"]`.

The continuing project is an inherited, **synthetic banking/payments brownfield** worked by a team. No employer, account, workload volume, production environment, legal applicability, provider contract, or customer fact is inferred. Team evidence records role-based ownership rather than learner names or PII. Daily closure requires an artifact ID, owning role, measurable acceptance rule, and reviewer/replay evidence.

Personal `AWS` / `GCP` / `Both` reading selection remains separate from the team's single Week 6 primary-cloud ADR. The selected provider is the learner's primary reading plane; the other provider is comparison depth. The ADR controls team implementation examples and is never rewritten by a personal picker.
