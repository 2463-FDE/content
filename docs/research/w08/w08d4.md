# w08d4 — Reliability guardrails, evaluations, and graceful degradation

**Verified:** 2026-10-14  
**Authority:** provisionally authoritative LTM derivative, Week 8 THU; reviewed prior evaluation and guardrail research preserved in the archive.

## Source-assigned scope

Input validation, output filtering, circuit breakers, graceful degradation, contextual-grounding checks, provider-independent guardrail wrapping, and failure drills. This reliability treatment does not duplicate Week 9 security hardening.

## Reviewed claims

- Circuit breakers prevent repeated calls to a failing dependency and require explicit open, half-open and closed behavior; retries must be bounded and use backoff. They improve containment, not correctness. — [AWS Builders' Library: timeouts, retries and backoff](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/)
- Offline evaluations use labeled fixtures and reference outputs where available; online evaluations sample production-shaped runs and generally use reference-free signals. Production examples should feed the versioned offline set. — [LangSmith evaluation concepts](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/evaluation-concepts)
- LLM-as-judge findings are measurements that need calibration and bias controls. The archived PR12-reviewed research dates the >80% agreement and 66/80 position-bias findings to 2023 and does not present them as current-model guarantees. — [`archive-observability-v1/w07d2.md`](archive-observability-v1/w07d2.md)
- A guardrail result belongs beside trace, model, tool and evaluation signals with policy version and action: allow, degrade, refuse, retry or escalate. A refusal can be the correct reliable outcome.
- Graceful degradation needs a user-safe response and operating evidence. Silently omitting data or returning an unsupported answer is not graceful.

## Product boundary

Provider guardrail APIs are examples in the authoritative source, not guarantees. The learner exercise uses deterministic synthetic scores and static policy decisions; no paid model or external service is called.
