## Source-assigned scope

OTel traces/metrics/logs; Collector and OTLP export; GenAI spans, agent spans and metrics; LangGraph/model-call instrumentation; model, embedding and retrieval drift; ADOT/CloudWatch/X-Ray versus Cloud Trace/Managed Service for Prometheus as an evidence-gated portability comparison.

## Reviewed claims

- OpenTelemetry defines traces, metrics and logs as signals and OTLP as its telemetry protocol. A Collector can receive, process and export telemetry, including fan-out to multiple backends. — [Signals](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/), [OTLP](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/otlp/), [Collector](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/collector/)
- W3C Trace Context standardizes `traceparent` and `tracestate` for cross-service propagation. Business IDs remain separate from trace context. — [W3C Trace Context](https://www.w3.org/TR/trace-context/)
- The OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions are in a dedicated repository and remain Development status. Model, agent and tool operations need pinned instrumentation; names may change. — [GenAI conventions repository](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai), [Model spans](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai/blob/main/docs/gen-ai/gen-ai-spans.md), [Agent spans](https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai/blob/main/docs/gen-ai/gen-ai-agent-spans.md)
- GenAI content fields can contain sensitive information and are opt-in; useful correlation does not require exporting prompts, outputs or retrieved documents by default.
- Drift is a hypothesis requiring a baseline, comparison window and response. Output distribution, embeddings and retrieval quality are different signals and do not prove one another.
