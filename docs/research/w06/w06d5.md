## Measurement contract

“Measured egress” in this repository means counted bytes from synthetic serialized records. A cost exists only when a trainer supplies a dated rate and direction/zone/region assumptions. Otherwise the report shows bytes and formula with cost marked pending. The exercise makes no traffic, charge, identity, or infrastructure change.
