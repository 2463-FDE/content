## Claims and first-party evidence

- Delivery semantics depend on producer, broker, consumer, and side-effect behavior rather than a label alone — [Apache Kafka design documentation](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#design).

- At-most-once, at-least-once, idempotent production, and transactional Kafka processing have bounded meanings; arbitrary external side effects are outside a Kafka transaction — [Apache Kafka design: delivery semantics](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#semantics) and [producer configuration](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#producerconfigs).
- Avro and registry-backed compatibility are event-contract tools; compatibility must be chosen and enforced — [Confluent Schema Registry fundamentals](https://docs.confluent.io/platform/current/schema-registry/fundamentals/index.html).
- Python client configuration, delivery reports, consumer polling, commits, and transactional APIs are documented by the client maintainer — [Confluent Kafka Python overview](https://docs.confluent.io/kafka-clients/python/current/overview.html).
- Lag is partition/group progress and rebalances move assignments; handlers must tolerate interruptions and redelivery — [Apache Kafka consumer design](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#consumerconfigs).
- Amazon MSK is a managed Apache Kafka service. Provisioned-cluster economics include broker-instance time and broker storage; connectivity options can add charges — [Amazon MSK overview](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/msk/latest/developerguide/what-is-msk.html) and [pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/msk/pricing/).
- Google Cloud Managed Service for Apache Kafka is Kafka-compatible and prices provisioned capacity using DCUs plus storage; Google's pricing page defines current unit and commitment details — [service overview](https://cloud.google.com/managed-service-for-apache-kafka/docs/overview) and [pricing](https://cloud.google.com/managed-service-for-apache-kafka/pricing).
- Google Cloud Pub/Sub is a separate messaging product and API, not a Kafka-compatible endpoint — [Pub/Sub overview](https://cloud.google.com/pubsub/docs/overview).

## Economics contract

Elicit partitions, ingress/egress, retention, zones, availability, connectivity, growth, and operating ownership. Preserve provider-native units. Do not equate a DCU to a broker or invent a total. A dated trainer scenario may supply rates later; no live cluster or billing action is permitted.
