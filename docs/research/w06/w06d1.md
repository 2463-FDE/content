## Claims and first-party evidence

- Kafka models topics as partitioned logs; offsets identify positions within a partition, and ordering is partition-scoped — [Apache Kafka introduction](https://kafka.apache.org/intro) and [design documentation](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#design).
- Producers choose records, keys, partitions, and acknowledgement behavior; consumers track partition progress — [Apache Kafka design documentation](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#design) and [protocol guide](https://kafka.apache.org/protocol.html).
- Consumers in one group divide partition ownership; additional groups consume independently — [Apache Kafka consumer design](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#consumerconfigs). Operational evidence should include lag and partition state — [Apache Kafka operations](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#operations).
- Brokers serve record traffic while KRaft controllers manage metadata consensus in current Kafka — [Apache Kafka KRaft documentation](https://kafka.apache.org/documentation/#kraft).
- Topic creation, production, consumption, and inspection are available in official CLI workflows; the lesson teaches intent and evidence, not command recall — [Apache Kafka quickstart](https://kafka.apache.org/quickstart).
