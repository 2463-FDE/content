Alternative Research

The Wednesday ritual. Every week you research the alternatives across that week's whole content space — the models, services, frameworks, and tools a Forward Deployed Engineer would actually weigh for a client. The win isn't picking the "best" tool; it's defending a recommendation against real constraints: cost, lock-in, maturity, and data residency.

🗓️ Each week has 4 alternatives and there are 4 of you — log in and you'll be assigned one. Research your assigned alternative this week, then present it to the group for ~10 minutes on Friday. Across four weeks the rotation puts everyone in every slot. Exception — Week 4 runs as a collective: no individual assignments; the group researches all options together and presents one shared comparison. 📄 See an example submission (Next.js vs Vue vs Angular)
