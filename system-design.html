Practice library

The one targeted challenge above is required. These are optional reps — generic system-design prompts you can whiteboard anytime to build the muscle. No project tie-in; just design and defend.

Rep 1 · LLM gateway

Design a rate-limited, multi-tenant LLM gateway: per-tenant token budgets, a fallback model on timeout/overload, structured-output validation, and cost/latency observability. Defend the failure modes — what happens when the primary model is down or a tenant blows its budget?

Rep 2 · Idempotent intake

Design an idempotent webhook/event receiver for a third-party source (payments, eligibility, lab results): dedup, at-least-once delivery, ordering, and replay. Where's the dedup key, and what does it cost you to get it wrong?

Rep 3 · RAG eval harness

Design a RAG eval harness from scratch: gold-set construction, recall / precision / groundedness metrics, and how you'd detect retrieval drift as the corpus changes over time. What would tell you the index is silently degrading?

Rep 4 · Append-only audit trail

Design an append-only audit/event log that can answer "who did what to record X, and when?" — schema, the immutability guarantee, retention, and an anomaly/breach-detection signal layered on top. Defend why app logs don't count.
